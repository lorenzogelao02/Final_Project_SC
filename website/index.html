Defining the "Survival Score"

Our project combines multiple factors—from drinking fountains to shelter violations—to create a unified metric for urban survival.

Accessibility (+)

Proximity to drop-in centers, public toilets, and charging kiosks.

Risk (-)

Shelter safety violations and localized drug-related incidents.
